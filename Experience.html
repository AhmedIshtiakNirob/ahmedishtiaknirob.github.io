Animal Welfare & Community Initiative

01 Animal Welfare Initiative

Founder & President

SAU Rescue Community (SAURC)
November 2024 – PresentSher-e-Bangla Agricultural UniversityStudent-led

SAU Rescue Community is a student-led animal welfare organization at Sher-e-Bangla Agricultural University dedicated to the care, treatment and well-being of campus dogs and cats. Our mission is to create a safe and compassionate environment through medical assistance, vaccinations, food and shelter while promoting responsible pet care and awareness among students and faculty. Through collaboration with veterinarians, volunteers and animal lovers, we are building a sustainable support system for stray animals on campus.

  • Animal rescue, treatment and rehabilitation
  • Free vaccination campaigns, including World Veterinary Day 2025
  • Food, shelter and medical assistance
  • Student-volunteer coordination and responsible animal-care awareness
