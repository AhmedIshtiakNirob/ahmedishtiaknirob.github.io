Field Intern Veterinarian

Department of Livestock Services (DLS) · LDDP Project · Internship
June 2025Dholaikhal Gorur Hat, Dhaka South City CorporationOn-site3-day field service
When3 consecutive days before Eid

SiteDholaikhal Gorur Hat, Dhaka

I was honored to be part of the Veterinary Medical Team assigned by DLS under the LDDP project during pre-Eid cattle market operations at Dholaikhal Gorur Hat, one of the busiest Qurbani haats in Dhaka.
