A three-day programme powered by GAIN, SUN, Global Platform Bangladesh, and SUN Youth Network Bangladesh, organized by IAAS Bangladesh SAU Dhaka in association with the Bangladesh Medical Students’ Society.

From thought-provoking keynotes to interactive project-based learning, the programme strengthened leadership skills, expanded understanding of sustainable food systems, and connected young changemakers.

Inspired to turn these learnings into action for a safer, more sustainable, and nutrition-secure future.
