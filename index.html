Veterinarian | M.S. Student in Microbiology | Researcher

Hello, I’m Dr. Ahmed Ishtiak Nirob, a veterinarian and M.S. student in Microbiology at Sher-e-Bangla Agricultural University, Bangladesh.

I completed my B.Sc. in Veterinary Science and Animal Husbandry with a CGPA of 3.54/4.00. My research interests include antimicrobial resistance, One Health, host–microbe interactions, microbiology and molecular biology, gut health, infectious and zoonotic diseases, microbial genomics, bioinformatics, and epidemiology.

My research experience spans veterinary, epidemiological, and infectious disease research, including systematic reviews and meta-analyses. I have practical experience in microbiology, molecular biology, epidemiological research, and data analysis using R and SPSS.

I am interested in pursuing interdisciplinary research in microbiology, antimicrobial resistance, infectious diseases, animal health, and One Health.
