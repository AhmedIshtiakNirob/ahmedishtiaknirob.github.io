Dr. Ahmed Ishtiak Nirob

Veterinarian | M.S. Student in Microbiology | Researcher

Hello, I’m Dr. Ahmed Ishtiak Nirob, a veterinarian and M.S. student in Microbiology at Sher-e-Bangla Agricultural University, Bangladesh. I completed my B.Sc. in Veterinary Science and Animal Husbandry with a CGPA of 3.54/4.00. My research interests include antimicrobial resistance, One Health, host–microbe interactions, microbiology and molecular biology, gut health, infectious and zoonotic diseases, microbial genomics, bioinformatics, and epidemiology.

My research experience spans veterinary, epidemiological, and infectious disease research, including systematic reviews and meta-analyses. I have practical experience in microbiology, molecular biology, epidemiological research, and data analysis using R and SPSS. Alongside research, I have been teaching Biology since 2018 to students from different academic backgrounds.

I am interested in pursuing interdisciplinary research in microbiology, antimicrobial resistance, infectious diseases, animal health, and One Health. I have also achieved an IELTS Academic score of 7.5/9.0 (Listening 8.0, Reading 7.5, Writing 6.5, Speaking 7.0). My long-term goal is to pursue advanced research training and contribute to meaningful scientific and public-health research.
